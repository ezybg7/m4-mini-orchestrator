# Screen research — lists & recipes (group "lists-recipes")

_2026-09-04 · read-only against `main` (dfcd0f0) · companion data: `lists-recipes.json`_

Method per screen: what Ambry does today (counts read from code), 3–5 apps whose equivalent screen does the same job (one URL per claim), three materially different directions buildable with `src/components/` (Button, SearchField, SectionHeader, GroupedRow, SettingsRow, SwipeRow, OverflowMenu, BottomSheet, Chip, StockCap, Toast; gesture-handler + reanimated are installed). Principles applied: minimal interactions · no keyboard where a control will do · no duplicated headers · iOS grouped-list feel · Android-clean.

---

## 1. Lists tab — `src/app/(tabs)/lists.tsx` → `GroceryList`

**Today.** LargeTitle "Lists" → `Input` "Add to your list…" (≥2 chars: catalog suggestion rows + `Add "{term}"`) → TO BUY (category-ordered, no aisle headers) → IN YOUR CART (header ⋯ = Clear without restocking; footer `Restock N to Kitchen`) → SUGGESTED (out/low pantry rows, 8 + Show all) → recategorise sheet (12 chips + Cancel). Costs: typed add = focus + keystrokes + return; suggestion = 1 tap; tick = 1; remove = swipe + 1 (Undo toast); category = swipe + 2; restock = 1 + review; clear = 3. The keyboard is the primary add path; Suggested is the only control-first path and sits below the list.

**Apps.**
- **Apple Reminders (Groceries list)** — items auto-file into aisle sections; drag an item to another section, and the list "remembers your preference next time"; completed items hidden behind Show Completed / Clear. Borrow: *drag-to-recategorise with memory* (3 taps → 1 drag, and the categoriser learns) — https://support.apple.com/en-us/105086 ; hidden-completed model — https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/complete-items-iph3fb74d597/ios
- **AnyList** — "a live list of best guesses is always present under the main entry bar"; Recent Items and Favorites buttons with "Add All"; crossed-off items can be hidden, accidental cross-off can be guarded. Borrow: *Recent/Favorites as a tap surface* — https://thesweetsetup.com/apps/anylist-favorite-grocery-shopping-app/ ; https://help.anylist.com/topics/lists/crossing-off-and-deleting-items/
- **Bring!** — input bar at the bottom; a tapped item "will then move to Recently Used" (green tiles under the list, one tap to re-add); a catalog of category tiles below that; quantities typed inline ("2 kg apples"). Borrow: *Recently Used as the re-buy surface* — https://www.getbring.com/help-center-main-categories/items-lists
- **OurGroceries** — crossed-off items "move to the bottom of the list, below the 'Add an item…' row"; cross-off can require long-press/swipe to prevent accidents; "Delete all crossed-off items…" one button; every item ever entered is the master list. Borrow: *accidental-tick guard as a setting* — https://www.ourgroceries.com/user-guide
- **Listonic / Todoist** — checked items drop to the bottom; screen kept active while shopping (Listonic, https://listonic.com/features); completed hidden by default, "appear at the bottom of a section", uncheck restores (Todoist, https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/view-completed-tasks-in-todoist-J19h2s).

**Directions.**
1. **Aisles** — Reminders-native. Regions: LargeTitle (right: basket button "Restock 4" when bought>0) · SearchField-style add · sections PRODUCE / DAIRY & EGGS / … (only non-empty) · a single BOUGHT (4) group at the bottom, collapsed to one row "Show 4 bought" (Reminders/Todoist) with ⋯ Clear · SUGGESTED. Rows: tick · tile · name · cap; **long-press-drag a row onto another section** recategorises (gesture-handler, already installed) and the categoriser remembers the choice per household (the `householdFoods` learning already exists). Empty: cart glyph + one sentence. Borrowed: Reminders sections/drag/memory; Todoist hidden-completed. Cost after: category 3 → 1 drag; tick 1; restock 1 (header); clear 3. Trade-off: re-opens the 3b decision (headers were dropped because the list did not shrink — hiding bought rows restores the shrink); bought rows out of sight for a housemate; drag is a device-pass gesture.
2. **Buy again** — tap-first add (Bring!/AnyList). Regions: LargeTitle · **BUY AGAIN** strip of `Chip`s (last 12 names restocked or cleared, per-device ring buffer in AsyncStorage, plus out/low pantry names) · a collapsed "Add something else…" row that only opens the keyboard on tap · TO BUY · IN YOUR CART (unchanged) · SUGGESTED folds into BUY AGAIN. Empty: the strip alone is the empty state. Borrowed: Bring! Recently Used tiles; AnyList Recent/Favorites. Cost: recurring item 1 tap (was type + return); typed add +1 tap. Trade-off: the typed path — the most used today — gets one tap slower; a real "bought before" list wants a `grocery_history` (or `food_outcomes`) read rather than a device buffer.
3. **Cart bar** — bought rows leave the scroll. Regions: LargeTitle · add field · TO BUY · SUGGESTED · a **sticky footer bar** "In your cart · 6 · Restock" (`Button` primary + count); tapping the count opens a BottomSheet listing bought rows (tap to un-buy, ⋯ Clear without restocking, Cancel). Borrowed: OurGroceries/Listonic bottom placement inverted into a bar; Todoist restore-by-uncheck. Cost: tick 1; restock 1; un-buy 2 (was 1); clear 3. Trade-off: a housemate's ticks are less visible; one more sheet; the bar must yield to the keyboard.

## 2. Restock review — `src/app/grocery-restock.tsx` → `RestockReview` on `CaptureReviewList`

**Today.** ScreenHeader "Restock" (back locked while committing, swipe too) → summary "N bought items" · `Put everything in…` · `N of M` bulk tick → `GroupedRow`s: checkbox · name · category · location chip · "expires Sep 11 · 7d" → divider "Not adding 2 — tick to include" (unlinked/Other rows default-unchecked) → footer `Add N items to pantry` (hint when 0). Snapshot at open; pantry-first two-write commit; toast "Moved N items". Empty: basket glyph + copy; no locations: copy + "Add a location" (replaces to Pantry). Cost from Lists: 2 taps happy path; per-row location = chip + pick (2); bulk = pill + pick (2).

**Apps.**
- **Sortly (Receive items)** — "select the receiving folder, and enter the quantity for each item"; partial receive leaves the PO "Partially Received"; a confirmation summary after. Borrow: *partial receive as a first-class state* (the unticked rows staying bought is the same idea, unnamed) — https://help.sortly.com/hc/en-us/articles/21170610546715-Purchase-Orders
- **Grocy** — "Add all list items to stock" opens the purchase form "with the items in the shopping list pre-filled" (best-before, amount, location per product). Borrow: *pre-filled purchase, location + due date per line* — https://github.com/grocy/grocy-docs/blob/master/tutorials/food.md
- **NoWaste** — inventory lists per fridge/freezer/pantry, "move items between lists". Borrow: *destination-first grouping* — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nowaste-food-inventory-list/id926211004
- **Paprika 3** — grocery filters "To Buy / Bought / All"; pantry items "automatically unchecked when you add recipes… to your grocery list". Borrow: *the loop closes both ways* — https://www.paprikaapp.com/help/ios/
- **Todoist** — completed at the bottom, uncheck to restore (https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/view-completed-tasks-in-todoist-J19h2s).

**Directions.**
1. **Inline restock** — no pushed screen. IN YOUR CART rows gain a trailing location `Chip` and an "expires Sep 11" caption; unlinked rows show a dimmed "Skip" state (tap to include); the section footer `Add 6 to pantry` commits in place; `Put everything in…` moves into the section ⋯. Borrowed: Grocy pre-filled lines; Todoist restore. Cost: 2 → 1 tap. Trade-off: the Lists screen gets heavier and live (snapshot-at-open semantics go); a mid-commit lock has no screen to lock; the divider's legibility must survive as a row state.
2. **By destination** — the review groups rows under FRIDGE / PANTRY / FREEZER / NOT ADDING (the `suggestLocation` result becomes the section); swipe a row to move it one section (leading "Fridge", trailing "Pantry"), long-press-drag for the rest; per-section "Move all…" in the SectionHeader. Borrowed: NoWaste lists per storage; Sortly receiving folder. Cost: happy path 2; location fix 1 swipe (was 2). Trade-off: more headers on screen; a poor suggestion means many moves; the divider becomes a fourth section.
3. **Confirm sheet** — Restock opens a `BottomSheet`, not a screen: "Add 6 to your pantry" · three lines (4 to Fridge · 2 to Pantry · Skipping 1: dish soap) · primary `Add to pantry` · secondary `Review first…` (pushes today's screen) · Cancel (rule 7: it commits). Borrowed: Sortly confirmation summary; Grocy defaults. Cost: 2 taps, no push; review optional. Trade-off: expiry previews hidden unless reviewed; the sheet must list skips by name or it hides a decision.

## 3. Recipes tab — `src/app/(tabs)/recipes.tsx` (Cook · My recipes · Community)

**Today.** LargeTitle "Recipes" → `SegmentedControl` (last used remembered; default `mine`). *Cook*: ideas `Button` (primary when items expire) → "N recipes use items expiring" → YOU CAN MAKE / ALMOST THERE bordered cards (thumb, title, status line, missing chips, chevron); empty = 3 CTAs. *My recipes*: a second "My recipes" heading + sort glyph + `+ Add recipe` → Folders row → YOURS / HOUSEHOLD / FAVORITES `RecipeRowItem`s; no search. *Community*: `Input` + two 44pt square buttons (tags, sort) → active-tag chips → `CommunityCard` feed. Costs: open a recipe 1–2; add 2–3 (segment, button, sheet row); favourite from a list 2 + round trip; delete 4 + Alert; the headline feature (Cook) is not the landing.

**Apps.**
- **Paprika 3** — built-in smart categories "All Recipes, Most Recent, Top Rated, Favorites, Uncategorized" plus custom ones; search by name/ingredients/directions/notes; grid or list. Borrow: *smart lists instead of segments* — https://www.paprikaapp.com/help/ios/
- **Mela** — sidebar of All / Favorites / Want to Cook / Most Recent + categories + Feeds; search covers titles, ingredients, instructions, notes. Borrow: *one search across everything* — https://www.macstories.net/reviews/mela-an-elegant-and-innovative-recipe-and-cooking-app-for-iphone-ipad-and-mac/
- **SuperCook** — pantry page; "Missing one ingredient" as a named filter; max-ingredients / cuisine filters; "shows you what ingredients you're missing". Borrow: *makeability as a filter chip, not a section* — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/supercook-recipe-by-ingredient/id1477747816
- **Cookpad** — five tabs Home / Search / Create / Activity / You; the You tab separates Saved from Your Recipes; search moved off the feed into its own tab. Borrow: *own vs saved as sections of one "You" surface* — https://blog.cookpad.com/us/exciting-changes-in-the-cookpad-app/
- **Samsung Food** — Pinterest-style collections; pantry-based suggestions; communities — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/samsung-food-meal-planner/id1133637674

**Directions.**
1. **Cook is the tab** (critique R-B1). Regions: LargeTitle "Recipes" (right: magnifier → unified `SearchField` over own/household/favourites; ＋ → `AddRecipeSheet`) · chip row Under 30 min · Uses expiring · Favorites · YOU CAN MAKE / ALMOST THERE as `GroupedRow`s with time on the meta line · an inset LIBRARY group: My recipes 12 › · Folders 3 › · Community › · ideas as a tinted `Button` above the list. Empty: one primary (Browse community), one secondary (Add), ideas as text link. Borrowed: SuperCook filters; Mela one-search; Cookpad's separate Search. Cost: makeable recipe 1; My recipes 2 (was 1–2); add 2. Trade-off: My recipes demoted; segment memory gone; Community two taps away.
2. **Smart lists index** (Paprika/Mela). No segments: a `SearchField` then inset-grouped `SettingsRow`s — What can I make (5) › · Favorites (9) › · Recent · Folders (3) › · Household (4) › · Community › · Ideas ✨ — each pushing a plain list built on one `RecipeCard`. Cost: any list 1 tap, recipe 2; search from the tab. Trade-off: one more level for everything; the ranking stops being the first thing seen; the tab becomes a menu.
3. **One list, chips** (SuperCook/AnyList). One FlatList of every rankable recipe with a `SearchField` and a chip row All · Can make · Almost · Favorites · Folder… · Community (source switch, styled as the last chip); every row a `RecipeCard` with a makeability caption; header ＋ and ⋯ (Sort & filter sheet, folds Community's two square buttons into one). Cost: filter 1, open 1, Community 1. Trade-off: "Community" is a source, not a filter — one chip carrying two semantics; the sort sheet must merge both lists' options.

## 4. Recipe detail — `src/app/recipe/[id]/index.tsx`

**Today.** ScreenHeader (title, back; right: heart · folder · flag · pencil) → photo 16:9 → title (again) + `DifficultyRing` → meta (Serves · time · visibility · by author) → stars + "4.3 · 12 ratings" → attribution → description → tag chips → INGREDIENTS card (✓/○ · qty · name · link glyph) → STEPS card → `NutritionLabel` → YOUR RATING → `Start cooking` (pantry check → 3-button Alert). Costs: cook 1 (+1 Alert when missing); add missing to list only via that Alert (2); per-ingredient add none; rate = scroll + 1; five icon-only header controls; the title is drawn twice.

**Apps.**
- **NYT Cooking** — Recipe Box, "add ingredients to a built-in grocery list" with immediate feedback, private notes, ratings; the critique faults the ingredients/steps toggle ("knowledge in the head") and the hidden grocery-list destination. Borrow: *immediate add-to-list feedback that links to the list* — https://ixd.prattsi.org/2023/02/design-critique-nyt-cooking-ios-app/ ; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-quick-tasty-meals/id911422904
- **Mela** — source link, servings and time sit above the title; a Scale button; Grocery export has items "pre-selected… so users can uncheck items they already have". Borrow: *pre-selected missing items* — https://www.macstories.net/reviews/mela-an-elegant-and-innovative-recipe-and-cooking-app-for-iphone-ipad-and-mac/
- **Paprika 3** — tap an ingredient to cross it off; durations in directions "highlighted in blue; tap to start countdown"; scale; keep-screen-on. Borrow: *tappable durations* — https://www.paprikaapp.com/help/ios/
- **Crouton** — data blocks in "rounded rectangles" (prep times, ingredients, numbered directions); custom scale amounts; "copy just the ingredients you need to your list". Borrow: *block layout* — https://www.macstories.net/reviews/crouton-review-an-elegant-modern-recipe-manager-and-cooking-aid/ ; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crouton-recipe-manager/id1461650987
- **Mealime** — Cookware / Ingredients / Instructions tabs; "Done" marks the recipe cooked — https://support.mealime.com/article/73-viewing-and-cooking-recipes

**Directions.**
1. **Ingredients act** — header title "" (name once, in the body); the ✓/○ row is a control: tapping an absent row adds it to the list with an Undo toast; a sticky footer holds `Start cooking` and a tinted `Add 2 missing` so the 3-button Alert disappears; heart stays in the header, folder/flag/edit move into an `OverflowMenu`. Borrowed: Mela pre-selection; NYT immediate feedback; critique 5.1/5.2/5.7. Cost: add missing 1; per-ingredient 1; cook 1, no Alert. Trade-off: a row that is both status and button needs a clear affordance (a trailing "+", 44pt); the footer costs 60pt of every scroll.
2. **Two panes** — compact hero (photo 4:3, title, ring, meta, rating) → `SegmentedControl` Ingredients | Steps → steps as cards with detected-duration chips "12 min ▶" → sticky `Start cooking`. Borrowed: Mealime tabs; Paprika durations. Cost: unchanged taps, far less scrolling. Trade-off: NYT's own failure mode — switching panes to check a quantity mid-step; mitigate by inlining matched quantities into step text (Crouton), which is a parser later.
3. **Card stack** — every region an inset-grouped card in a fixed order: Hero · Actions (`SettingsRow`s: Start cooking › · Add missing to list (2) › · Serves 4 with a − + stepper that rescales parseable quantities) · Ingredients · Steps · Rating / Comments (spec 52) · Nutrition; header = back + heart + ⋯. Borrowed: Crouton blocks/scale; Paprika scale. Cost: every action 1 tap; scale 1 tap per step, no keyboard. Trade-off: scaling display-only `quantity_text` is partial ("2 eggs" scales, "a pinch" does not — ADR O1/O2 territory); a longer page.

## 5. Cook mode — `src/app/recipe/[id]/cook.tsx`

**Today.** ScreenHeader (title) → "N OF M DONE" → scroll of bordered step cards (○/✓ · STEP n · 19pt text; ingredient rows when a recipe has no steps) → `Done cooking` at the end → all-done sheet "How did it turn out?" (stars · Not now). Keep-awake. Costs: 1 tap per step; finish = scroll + 1; checkmarks are in-memory (lost on background); no ingredients, no timers, no stock consumption.

**Apps.**
- **Mela** — fullscreen, larger font, "all but the current steps are dimmed"; ingredients checkable beside the steps; "tap on a timed step to start a built-in timer"; several recipes in one cook mode. Borrow: *dim-the-rest + tap-a-time* — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mela-recipe-manager/id1548466041 ; https://mela.recipes/help/
- **Crouton** — "Focus on one recipe step at time"; "tap on ingredients within a step to get the quantity"; hands-free advance; a timer HUD "keeps the times front of mind". Borrow: *quantities inside the step* — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crouton-recipe-manager/id1461650987
- **Tasty** — "each step fills your entire screen. It won't skip ahead until you tap the screen again"; swipe back/forward. Borrow: *one step per screen* — https://www.wilx.com/2025/11/25/what-tech-tasty-app/
- **Mealime** — one instruction per screen in "large, easy to read font"; wave to advance; "tap Done to mark the recipe as cooked". Borrow: *Done as an outcome, not a dismissal* — https://support.mealime.com/article/88-how-to-use-cooking-mode
- **Paprika 3 / Kitchen Stories** — detected timers, cross-off ingredients, keep screen on (https://www.paprikaapp.com/help/ios/); "built-in timers are placed exactly where you need them" (https://pages.kitchenstories.com/en/app).

**Directions.**
1. **Focused checklist** — keep the scroll; the first unchecked step is full-ink, the rest dimmed; a collapsed "Ingredients (8)" disclosure at the top; durations parsed from step text become `Chip`s "12 min ▶" that run an in-app timer (expo-keep-awake already on; a local notification on finish); a sticky footer `Finish (4 of 9)`; checks persisted per recipe in AsyncStorage and cleared on Finish. Borrowed: Mela dimming/timers; Paprika detection; critique 9.1–9.3. Cost: 1 tap per step; timer 1. Trade-off: duration parsing is a heuristic ("until golden" has none); a footer over a scroll on a propped-up phone.
2. **Step pager** — one step per page: "Step 3 of 9" · 24pt text · that step's ingredients (name-matched via the grocery normaliser) with quantities · big Back / Next (bottom, 56pt) and swipe · Done on the last page. Borrowed: Tasty/Mealime/Crouton. Cost: 1 tap or swipe per step, no scrolling. Trade-off: no overview; ingredient-to-step matching is fuzzy; a step that spans two screens of text still scrolls.
3. **Cook & consume** — the finish sheet becomes two stacked cards: stars, then "Used anything up?" chips of the recipe's in-stock ingredients (tap → Low, tap again → Out, via the existing status cycle) and `Done`. Borrowed: Mealime Done-as-cooked; the NOTES-2026-07-30 "cooking never consumes stock" gap. Cost: +1 tap per consumed item, optional. Trade-off: needs its own spec (SPEC.md says so); a longer sheet at the moment the user wants to eat; risk of nagging.

## 6. Editor, import, ideas — `recipe/new.tsx`, `recipe/[id]/edit.tsx`, `recipe/import.tsx`

**Today.** `RecipeForm`: Title (autofocus) → Photo (200pt dashed box; Alert chooser) → Servings (number pad) → Time (two number pads + Clear) → Difficulty slider → Tags (chips + `Choose tags…` + free text) → Description (single line) → Ingredients (rows: name `Input` · trash · "→ linked name" · amount `Input` · Optional chip · ▲▼) + "Add ingredient…" type-ahead → Steps (numbered `Input`s + `+ Add step`) → Visibility (3 radio cards) → `Create recipe` → Delete. ≈45–50 actions for a modest recipe; keyboard on servings/time/tags/description. *Import*: header → RECIPE LINK `Input` → `Import` → expectation copy (photo arm: two buttons) → the same form prefilled ("From smittenkitchen.com", Public disabled). *Ideas*: `BottomSheet` from Cook → cards (title · summary · chips · See method · `Save as draft`) → Regenerate → "Saved to My recipes" toast, then 2 more taps to reach the draft.

**Apps.**
- **Paprika 3** — Download button in the browser; a clipboard toolbar to "manually select recipe sections (name, ingredients, directions, photos) and assign them to fields"; expects "quantity unit ingredient". Borrow: *paste-to-field* — https://www.paprikaapp.com/help/ios/
- **Mela** — ingredient parsing recognises "amount and unit"; `#` markers group ingredients; markdown steps; scan a cookbook page and "drag on the scanned document to choose text blocks". Borrow: *free-text ingredients parsed per line* — https://mela.recipes/help/
- **Crouton** — copy a URL and "you'll see a pop-up offering to import that recipe"; photo/AI import "with no manual entry required"; smart tag suggestions. Borrow: *clipboard-aware entry* — https://www.macstories.net/reviews/crouton-review-an-elegant-modern-recipe-manager-and-cooking-aid/ ; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crouton-recipe-manager/id1461650987
- **Notion** — content is blocks; on mobile "tap the + button above the keyboard to add new blocks"; "Turn into" converts a block. Borrow: *optional fields as blocks you add, not fields you skip* — https://www.notion.com/help/what-is-a-block

**Directions.**
1. **Essentials first** (critique 6.1–6.9, Notion blocks). Order: Title → Ingredients → Steps → a "More details" disclosure holding `SettingsRow`s: Add a photo › (BottomSheet chooser), Serves − 4 + (stepper), Time as `Chip`s 15 · 30 · 45 · 1 h · 1 h 30 · 2 h · More… (wheel sheet), Difficulty slider, Tags = recent chips + More…, Description multiline → Visibility (remembers last non-public) → sticky `Save` above the keyboard accessory. Cost: a modest recipe ≈ title + ingredient adds + steps + Save; servings/time 1–2 taps, zero keyboard. Trade-off: two-level form; import review must still surface the linked names near the top.
2. **Paste anything** (Paprika toolbar, Mela parsing, Crouton prompt). New recipe opens on a clipboard-aware first row (`src/lib/clipboard.ts` exists): a URL → "Import from smittenkitchen.com" one tap; plain text → "Paste recipe text" → server text arm → prefilled form; ingredients get a multiline "Paste your ingredient list" box split per line by the shared `ingredientLine.ts` with per-line link `Chip`s. Cost: import 1 tap (was 3); 10 ingredients = 1 paste (was 10 type-ahead adds). Trade-off: iOS paste banner on clipboard reads; text→structure quality is the AI arm's, metered; two ways to add an ingredient.
3. **Review, don't edit** — an import (and a saved idea) lands on a read-only review card: title · "8 ingredients, 5 linked" with tappable "→ Cream" chips to relink · "6 steps" · `Save` primary · `Edit first` secondary; a saved idea opens this same card instead of toasting. Borrowed: Paprika download → Save; Crouton "save the bits you need"; Mela preview. Cost: import save 1 tap after the fetch; idea → editable recipe 2 (was 3). Trade-off: the D3 review gate must stay real — links are the one thing the card has to expose; two routes into `RecipeForm`.

## 7. Creator profile — `src/app/profile/[id]/index.tsx`

**Today.** ScreenHeader (name; right: flag) → initials avatar 72 → name (again) → "N recipes · 0 followers · 0 following" (inert until spec 53) → bio → link rows (label · host · ↗) → `CommunityCard`s without byline → "No public recipes yet." Spec 53 adds a Follow button (accent-outlined ⇄ filled), tappable counts, and an All/Following scope on Community. Cost: profile 1 tap from any byline; follow 1; report 3.

**Apps.**
- **Instagram** — photo · name · 150-char bio · up to five links shown as the first link "and N others" · posts/followers/following as tappable counts · grid tabs. Borrow: *collapsed link list; counts as buttons* — https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-link-in-bio/ ; https://help.instagram.com/customer/portal/articles/95730-view-list-of-followers-following
- **Pinterest** — "profile photo, name, bio and optionally, location"; saved boards vs created; only you see your secret boards. Borrow: *created vs saved split* — https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/find-your-profile
- **Strava** — identity header, an overview with tabs "Trophy Case, list of Followers, KOMs"; another athlete's page shows "essential stats… you can quickly see how you compare". Borrow: *stats relative to the viewer* — https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15402175-your-strava-profile-page
- **Cookpad** — follow from the profile; Saved / Your Recipes / Cooksnaps sections; following puts their recipes in your feed. Borrow: *profile explains what following buys* — https://blog.cookpad.com/uk/build-your-community-on-cookpad-followers-and-following/
- **Substack** — an app-profile Follow button; bio plus subscriber count "if you publish"; estimated counts above 1,000. Borrow: *counts only where they mean something* — https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/18261513315348-How-does-following-work-on-Substack

**Directions.**
1. **Card + grid** (Instagram/Pinterest). Header title "" until scrolled; a header card: avatar · name · three stat buttons (Recipes · Followers · Following) · bio · first link + "and 2 more" (sheet) · full-width tinted `Follow` / bordered `Following`; recipes as a two-column photo grid (compact `RecipeCard`, fallback tile). Cost: follow 1; recipe 1; extra links 2. Trade-off: many recipes lack photos, so the grid is half tiles; the stat buttons are dead until spec 53 lands.
2. **Compare & cook** (Strava/SuperCook). The stats line becomes "You can make 3 of Ana's 12 recipes"; a chip row All · Can make · Almost above the list; rows carry the Cook status line; `Follow` compact in the header right slot. Cost: follow 1; filter 1. Trade-off: needs ingredients for a stranger's public recipes (FEED_SELECT has none — a capped second query) and a small matching spec; a heavier first paint.
3. **Contact card** (Settings idiom). No name in the header; inset groups: identity row (avatar · name · `Follow` as the accessory) · LINKS as `SettingsRow`s (label, host, open glyph) · FOLLOWERS 12 › / FOLLOWING 4 › rows · RECIPES (12) as dense `RecipeRowItem`s with a ⋯ sort. Cost: everything 1 tap; report in ⋯. Trade-off: reads like Settings, not like a creator page; photos shrink to 44pt.

## What I would bet on
