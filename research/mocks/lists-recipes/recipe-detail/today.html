Sunday-morning pancakes from the test kitchen.
BreakfastQuick
Ingredients
1 cup Flour
2 Eggs
1 cup Milk
2 tbsp Butter
Steps
1.Whisk the flour and eggs together.
2.Add the milk, then rest the batter for 10 minutes.
3.Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
