# Ambry monetization plan (condensed from the 2026-09-04 research memo; the full memo was lost with the session scratchpad — key claims to re-verify: comparable-app prices, Apple's +50% price-increase consent rule, RevenueCat single-offering/trial guidance)

One paid tier: **Ambry Plus** ($4.99/mo · $29.99/yr · 14-day trial recommended; locked spec says $2.99/$24.99/7 days).
Free forever (zero marginal cost): manual add/type-ahead/CRUD/batches; barcode + OFF (unmetered — fix L9: barcode_fetch must not count against the 20/day kind-agnostic counter); expiry/urgency/Expiring; locations/zones/search; grocery/restock/collab; household sharing (members 2 → 3); recipes CRUD + photos (500) + cook mode; deterministic matching; community browse/save/ratings/folders AND publishing; scorecard; offline cache; dark mode; digest + customization; shelf-life AI estimates (uncharged).
Free AI taste: 8 scans + 3 ideas per calendar month, plus a one-time welcome grant of 10 scans + 3 ideas (never expiring, spent first; ~$0.10/install); daily abuse cap 20 flat.
Plus: 300 scans/mo ("unlimited" with fair use), 100 ideas/mo, unlimited members in owned households, photos 500 → 2000, priority shelf-life learning (household corrections apply immediately), themes/accent palettes (JS; alternate app icons need a native build), rich export (basic CSV/Markdown export stays free), later: home-screen widget (native), forward-looking insights (never a multi-month waste trend — spec 39's decision stands).
Lapse contract (write into spec 23): all data stays readable/editable; export stays; households over the cap are grandfathered (join gate only fires on join); photos above the ceiling never deleted; account deletion works regardless and says it does not cancel the Apple subscription.
Cost basis: Haiku 4.5 vision ≈ $0.008/scan (≤1568 visual tokens), ideas ≈ $0.006 (the spec's 1.5–2¢ Sonnet figure is stale), shelf-life up to 20 model calls per ai_calls row and no monthly quota; ceiling-riding subscriber ≈ $3/mo model spend vs ≈ $0.30 realistic.
Implementation: migrations 0061 ai_welcome_grant (+ claim_ai_call spends the grant inside its advisory lock; signup trigger), 0062 household_free_member_cap (3), 0063 claim_ai_call fetch-kind daily split (L9); Worker: scanQuota adds grant_remaining/grant_ideas_remaining, MAX_PHOTOS_PER_USER plan-dependent, priority learning in shelfLife catalog-write path, QUOTA_FREE_MONTHLY 8 (env + code defaults in receipt.ts/scanQuota.ts); client: quotaDisplay grant states, first-run one-liner naming the grant, paywall benefit lines from the server + the 3.1.2 price-prominence fix (billed price must be the most prominent element; the savings badge is louder today), STATIC_PACKAGES matching ASC, Profile rows (themes, export), export via share sheet from the cache, three paywall entrances only (no nags). Specs: monetization.md (§Product model, §Cost model, new §Lapse contract, §Quota semantics), ADR D1 (grant, cap 3, barcode constraint corrected) + D2 (price/trial) revisions, app-store-release §D (3.1.2 check, Small Business Program 15-day lag).
